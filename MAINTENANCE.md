locking and atomic persistence are implemented and covered by repository tests.
Current CI facts belong to the exact validated HEAD, not historical test counts.
Native GPT research can run successfully while operational quant scoring remains
`max(0, daily_return)`; it does not set weights, quantities or prices.

- Acquire reviewed inception/horizon prices with complete corporate-action
  coverage and immutable receipts. Public downloads alone are insufficient.
- Preserve missing/unresolved outcomes; never manufacture a mature return.
- Never confuse legacy symbol/horizon rows with independent portfolio samples.
- Keep canonical runtime and the separate alpha lab isolated. Broker submission,
  automatic promotion and real trading remain disabled.

  grid are implemented (ADR 0036). Collect qualified evidence before financial
  claims; continuous buy-and-hold, integer fills and intra-horizon paths remain
  outside this hypothetical model.

  absence must contribute zero and remain visible.
- Extend reviewed calendar coverage beyond 2026–2028 with official exceptional
  closures, action metadata and historical universe membership evidence.
- Add authenticated review governance if local manual attestation is insufficient.
  Current receipts are not digital signatures.
- Complete measured provider/research/ledger/publication soak telemetry; fixture
  timings are not production SLOs or continuous live observation.

## P2 — operational review

- Use decision attribution to study actual NO_ACTION causes: quant signal, risk
  reductions, missing limit-price inputs, cash/whole-share/minimum notional and
  projected-portfolio gates. Do not infer historical frequency.

- The public-history pattern audit found historical personal paths; assess
  privacy manually. No automatic visibility change or history rewrite.
